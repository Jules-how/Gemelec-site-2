🏷️ Compliance

Test & Tag

Minimise risk and meet compliance requirements.

Keep your workplace compliant with Australian Standard AS/NZS 3760. We test and tag all portable electrical appliances and equipment — ideal for construction sites, offices, schools, hospitality venues, and events. Fast, thorough, and fully documented with printed registers provided.

🔧 Switchboard

Switchboard Repairs & Upgrades

Protect your property from electrical faults.

An outdated switchboard is one of the leading causes of electrical fires and insurance refusals in older Sydney homes. We upgrade ceramic fuses to modern circuit breakers and safety switches (RCDs), rewire panels, and install new switchboards for extensions, granny flats, and full renovations.
