What we install

CCTV & Security Services

01

IP Camera Network Design & Install

Custom camera placement designed to eliminate blind spots. High-resolution IP cameras with night vision, weatherproofing, and wide-angle coverage for every property type.

02

NVR / DVR Recording Systems

Network video recorders with local storage and cloud backup options. View live and recorded footage from your phone anywhere in the world.

03

Doorbell & Entry Cameras

Smart doorbell cameras including Ring, Nest, and Hikvision — professionally installed and integrated with your existing network for reliable, always-on coverage.

04

Alarm Systems & Sensors

Motion sensors, door and window contacts, glass break detectors, and siren units — designed to deter intruders and alert you immediately if triggered.

05

Concealed Cable Management

All cabling run inside walls and ceiling cavities wherever possible for a clean finish. No surface-mounted cable trays or messy runs across your property.

06

Remote Access Setup & Training

Full network configuration so you can access live feeds and recordings from your phone or computer. We walk you through the system before we leave.
