Our story

Who We Are

GemElec was founded with one goal: deliver electrical work that Sydney families and businesses can genuinely rely on. We're not a large faceless contractor — we're a tight-knit, family-run team where every job matters and every client is treated like a neighbour.

Operating out of Matraville in Sydney's south-east, we've spent more than 15 years building a reputation on quality workmanship, straight talk, and showing up when we say we will. From a quick fault fix to a full commercial fit-out, the standard never drops.

We work across residential and commercial properties throughout Greater Sydney — from the Eastern Suburbs and Inner South to the Inner West and beyond. Whether it's a late-night emergency or a planned upgrade, you'll always speak to someone who knows the job.
